SAMPLE DATA - THE CAST AND THE GAGS

The reference for every character and company in sample_data.json. All fictional, all
parody: characters from cult action and sci-fi films and shows, at clients that are light
twists on each film's own fictional firm. Every email is a TEST address (test.<company>.com)
and every phone a 555-01nn number, so nothing is ever delivered to anyone.

Rules for adding to it (see ../CLAUDE.md):
  - Fictional characters only, with a first and last name. No real people.
  - Semi-cult and well liked over the most famous; no Star Wars, little Star Trek.
  - The humour is in what they are tracked doing, kept professional.
