APP-OWNED PRE-LAUNCH CHECKLIST CONVENTION
Date: 2026-07-30

SUMMARY
    RSpade now standardizes WHERE a downstream app keeps its OWN pre-launch audit
    checklist: rsx/resource/audits/prelaunch_checklist.md. This is a one-time
    convention/organization adoption - no code changes, no behavior change. It
    exists so every RSpade app puts its application-specific launch audits in the
    same, framework-documented place, distinct from the framework-required audits
    that ship in `rsx:man prelaunch_checklist`.

    Two lists, two owners:
    - FRAMEWORK-required pre-launch audits (hard-to-lint, framework-wide) live in
      `php artisan rsx:man prelaunch_checklist`. These arrive automatically via
      `rsx:framework:pull` and grow over time as new entries.
    - APPLICATION-specific pre-launch audits (your permission gates, your email
      templates, your seed-data cleanup, etc.) live in
      rsx/resource/audits/prelaunch_checklist.md, owned and maintained by you.

    Review BOTH before every launch.

THE CHANGE
    - New convention: rsx/resource/audits/prelaunch_checklist.md is the home for
      your app's own pre-launch audit items. The template ships a short starter
      file there with example bullets to replace.
    - The convention and the "review both lists" guidance are now documented in
      the framework CLAUDE you receive (internally docs.dist/CLAUDE.dist.md), so it
      is part of the standard programmer-facing guidance from here on.

ACTION REQUIRED
    1. If you already maintain a pre-launch audit list of your own (in a scratch
       doc, a README, notes in your CLAUDE.md, wherever), MOVE it to
       rsx/resource/audits/prelaunch_checklist.md.

    2. Because the file's existence and purpose are now documented in the
       framework CLAUDE guidance, you MAY remove any notes in your own CLAUDE.md
       that merely explained "we keep a pre-launch checklist" - that is now
       covered for you.

       BUT PRESERVE any special directives or notes of yours that DIFFER FROM or
       EXTEND the framework guidance (app-specific launch policy, required
       sign-offs, environment-specific steps). Move those into the new checklist
       file; do not lose them.

    3. Keep reviewing the framework-required audits at
       `php artisan rsx:man prelaunch_checklist` as well. Review BOTH before
       launch.

    Note: this is a one-time convention adoption. FUTURE framework pre-launch
    audit items arrive automatically as new entries in `rsx:man prelaunch_checklist`
    - there is NO per-item upstream_changes doc for those. This doc exists only to
    establish the app-owned-checklist location once.

VERIFICATION
    - rsx/resource/audits/prelaunch_checklist.md exists and contains your app's
      pre-launch items (starter examples replaced with your own).
    - `php artisan rsx:man prelaunch_checklist` still renders the framework audits.
    - Any app-specific launch directives that previously lived in your CLAUDE.md
      are now in the checklist file (nothing lost in the move).
